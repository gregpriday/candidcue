## What happens after a refund

Your registration is cancelled.

Because CandidCue has no licence key, no activation and no licence check, there
is nothing to hand back and nothing that switches off. The application on your
machine keeps working exactly as it did — see section 6 of the
[EULA](EULA.md). Your scripts and settings are yours and stay on your device.

What changes is the licence, not the software. After a refund you are no longer
registered, so continuing to use CandidCue as a regular tool would require
registering again. We are trusting you on that, in the same way the rest of this
arrangement trusts you.

**Agreeing to that does not affect the 30-day promise above.** The statutory
right and our voluntary guarantee are separate things, and the voluntary one is
