## What happens after a refund

Your Registration ends. You are no longer registered, so continuing to use
CandidCue as a regular tool would mean registering again. We are trusting you on
that, in the same way the rest of this arrangement trusts you.

Issuing a refund does not send any command to the application, alter it, or
disable it remotely. Because CandidCue has no licence key, no activation and no
licence check, there is nothing to hand back and nothing that switches off — see
section 6 of the [EULA](EULA.md). Your scripts and settings are yours and stay
on your device.

What changes is your Registration and your right to use CandidCue as a regular
tool — not the software, and not your underlying permission to have it
installed.
