## Why this matters

I read these names out loud on purpose. A tool that follows your voice has to survive real names, said at speed, by someone who cares about saying them correctly. If it can keep its place through Nkosinathi, Siobhán, Przemysław, Ximena, Thandiwe, Joaquín, Saoirse, Ruaridh, Tsholofelo and Dagný, then your quarterly all-hands is going to be just fine.

The roadmap itself is short. Ship the mobile companion, finish the audit trail, and get out of the way. Everything else on the list is detail, and the people I just named will handle the detail better than any slide I could show you.
