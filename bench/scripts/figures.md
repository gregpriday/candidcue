# The numbers pass

Right, this one is deliberately horrible. It is a page of figures read out loud at normal speed, because that is the shape of script the tracker is worst at and the shape nobody ever tests.

Here is why it matters. When I say a number, the recogniser has to decide how to write it down, and it does not always decide the way the script did. I write twenty-seven and it writes 27. I write 27 and it writes twenty-seven. Both of us are right and the display still has to land on the same word.

## Where the quarter landed

We finished the quarter with twenty-seven thousand active accounts. That is up from nineteen thousand four hundred at the end of March, so call it forty percent growth across ninety-one days, which is the best stretch this company has had.

Revenue for the year came in at 2.4 million dollars. The plan said 2.1 million dollars, so we are ahead, and I want to be careful about how much credit we take for that because roughly a third of it arrived in the last six weeks and none of us saw it coming.

Support handled one hundred and forty-three escalations last month. One hundred and twenty of those closed inside a day. The median first response was 1.2 seconds on chat and just under four hours on email, which is the gap we are fixing next.

## The numbers I am asked for most

Pricing has not moved. It is still $40 a month for a seat, still 60% off for annual, and still free under three seats. We said that in 1998 terms it would be about eight dollars, which is a joke that has never once landed.

The infrastructure bill is 1.6 million dollars a year. Egress alone is one hundred and eighteen thousand of that. We audit it every quarter and the last audit was 2019, which tells you something about the word every.

We record at 24kHz, mono, sixteen bits a sample. The first partial lands in about 900 milliseconds and the final about 1.7 seconds after you stop talking. Version 3.2 cut both of those by roughly a fifth.

## Dates, times and the rest

The beta opens Tuesday, the twenty-eighth of July. The all-hands is at three thirty in the afternoon, the recording goes up at nine fifteen the next morning, and the embargo lifts at 9am on the Monday after that.

Our first office opened in 1905 and burned down in 1998, which are two facts I keep in the same sentence because nobody believes either of them alone. We moved into the current building in 2019 and signed for another six years in 2024.

That is the whole page. Twenty-seven, one hundred and forty-three, 2.4 million, 1.2 seconds, twenty-eighth of July, three thirty, 24kHz, 1905. If the words on the wall stayed with me through all of that, the feature works.
