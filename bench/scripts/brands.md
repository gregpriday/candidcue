# Names, brands and other things nobody spells right

This is the other half of the hard problem. Numbers are hard because there are two ways to write them. Names are hard because there are two ways to hear them, and the recogniser picks one of them without telling you.

## The partners

The integration list is long and I am going to read all of it. We ship with RustLight Games, who were first and still send us the best bug reports. Then SoundCloud, PagerDuty, OpenTable and BrightHarbour, in the order they signed. LumenWorks came later and immediately became the biggest by volume.

Half of those are one word on the page and two words in the transcript. The recogniser hears RustLight and writes Rust Light. It hears CandidCue and writes Candid Cue. It is not wrong, exactly, and the display still has to stay on the line.

## The teams and the places

Our engineering team sits in Cape Town, with a second group in Stellenbosch and one person in Ōtautahi who insists the time zone is a feature. Jean-Luc Bertrand runs platform. Anne-Marie Okonkwo runs everything that touches billing. Between them they have shipped more of this product than the rest of us put together.

The research group is a different animal. Kwabena Asare-Boateng and Ingrid Sørensen have been arguing about the same latency budget for two years and the argument keeps making the product better, so nobody is stopping them.

## The alphabet soup

We publish an API, we ship an SDK, and the whole thing is sold as SaaS, which means every second sentence in this company is an acronym. The R&D budget sits under the same approval chain as AT&T used to, which is a story for another day.

The training rig is eight H100 cards, and the laptops are M2 Max, and the render boxes still run RTX 4090s because replacing them is nobody's favourite quarter. IPO, KPI, SLA, GDPR. I can say all of those out loud without flinching now and I am not proud of it.

## Why I read this out

None of this is showing off. A teleprompter that loses its place on a partner list is a teleprompter you stop trusting, and partner lists are exactly where you cannot afford to be scrolling with your eyes. RustLight, SoundCloud, PagerDuty, OpenTable, BrightHarbour, LumenWorks, CandidCue. Cape Town, Stellenbosch, Ōtautahi. Jean-Luc, Anne-Marie, Kwabena, Ingrid. If the line holds through that, it holds through anything I am going to say on a stage.
