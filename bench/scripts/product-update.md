# Quarterly product update

Hey everyone, thanks for tuning in. I want to walk you through what we shipped this quarter and where we're headed next.

The headline is the new capture pipeline. Recording used to take four separate steps, and honestly, most people gave up halfway through. Now you press one button and the whole thing just happens. Behind the scenes we rebuilt the encoder, moved processing onto a background thread, and cut export times roughly in half.

The second big change is collaboration. You can now share a draft with your team before anything goes public. They see exactly what you see, they can leave comments on specific moments, and nothing gets published until you say so. This was the single most requested feature in our survey, and it feels great to finally hand it over.

We also spent a lot of time on the boring stuff that matters. Crash reports are down sixty percent. Cold start is under two seconds on most machines. The installer no longer asks you seven questions before letting you in. None of this makes a splashy demo, but it's the difference between software you tolerate and software you trust.

Looking ahead, the next quarter is about the mobile companion. The idea is simple: review and approve from your phone, record from your desk. We're testing it internally right now, and the early feedback has been honest, which is a polite way of saying brutal. That's fine. Better we hear it than you.

One last thing. Pricing isn't changing this year. We said that in January and people keep asking, so I'll say it again: no changes, no surprises, nothing hidden in the fine print.

Thanks for watching, and as always, tell us what's broken. We read everything.
